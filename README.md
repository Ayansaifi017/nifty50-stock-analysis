# nifty50-stock-analysis
End-to-end stock market analysis of 15 Nifty 50 companies using Python and Excel — Sep 2025 to Sep 2026

# Nifty 50 Stock Performance Analysis
### Python + Excel | Sep 2025 – Sep 2026 | 15 Companies | 3,688 Trading Records

---

## Project Overview

End-to-end analysis of daily stock price data for 15 Nifty 50 companies 
over a one-year period (September 2025 to September 2026).

Python was used to collect real market data automatically via the Yahoo Finance API.  
Excel was used for data cleaning, PivotTable analysis, and dashboard creation.

**Companies analyzed:** Reliance, TCS, Infosys, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Wipro,

Hindustan Unilever, Larsen & Toubro, Axis Bank

---

## Key Findings

| # | Finding |
|---|---------|
| 1 | Only **4 of 15 stocks** generated positive period returns — 11 declined |
| 2 | **Sun Pharma** was the top performer at **+15.91%** period return |
| 3 | **Tata Motors** saw the steepest decline at **−57.41%** |
| 4 | **March 2026** was the sharpest broad-based decline month — 14 of 15 stocks fell vs. February |
| 5 | **April 2026** showed recovery — 11 of 15 stocks posted higher monthly average closes |
| 6 | **HDFC Bank** recorded the highest average daily trading volume at **~31.46 million shares/day** — more than double Wipro at 16.23 million |
| 7 | **Tata Motors** had the highest intraday return variability at **1.63%** standard deviation |
